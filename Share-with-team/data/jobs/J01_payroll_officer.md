J01 — Payroll Officer Job Specification

Company: Melbourne Business Services 

Location: Melbourne, Victoria, Australia

Employment type: Full-time

Position Overview

Melbourne Business Services is seeking a Payroll Officer to manage employee payroll processing, maintain accurate payroll records and ensure compliance with relevant Australian employment requirements.

The successful candidate will be responsible for processing fortnightly payroll, calculating employee wages, maintaining employee records, resolving payroll discrepancies and assisting with payroll reporting.

ESSENTIAL CRITERIA

E1: At least two years of experience processing employee payroll, including calculating wages, maintaining payroll records and resolving payroll discrepancies.

E2: Certificate IV in Accounting and Bookkeeping, Diploma of Accounting, or equivalent relevant qualification or experience.

E3: Intermediate Microsoft Excel skills, including formulas, VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP and pivot tables.

E4: Demonstrated ability to maintain accurate financial records, identify discrepancies and perform payroll reconciliations.

DESIRABLE CRITERIA

D1: Experience using payroll software such as MYOB, Xero or KeyPay.

D2: Knowledge of Australian Modern Awards and the National Employment Standards (NES).
